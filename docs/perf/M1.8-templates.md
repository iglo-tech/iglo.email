peak-heap or CPU-preemption guarantees, and they are not CI thresholds. The

## Compile-once renderer comparison

`bun test/templates/measure-render.ts` ran each implementation serially with
200 warmup recipients, then three 10,000-recipient repetitions. The exact M2.2
synthetic control and production owner use the same narrower shared subset:
HTML body and layout, plain body and subject, three array items, interpolation,
`for`, `upcase` and `raw`. Translation and tracking filters are excluded because
the control uses synthetic results that intentionally differ from the owner.
Both compile the sources once per repetition. The owner has no shared cache;
a caller may retain an immutable compiled snapshot. Parse time includes both
HTML and plain compilation, render time includes synthetic data construction,
and total includes assertions, digesting and bookkeeping. RSS is sampled every
100 recipients; it is not an observed peak guarantee.

| Mode | Repetition | Parse ms | Render ms | Total ms | Sampled RSS bytes |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Control | 1 | 22.852 | 367.983 | — |
| Control | 2 | 26.473 | 322.787 | — |
| Control | 3 | 23.531 | 295.008 | — |
| Owner | 1 | 1.195 | 977.009 | 1026.862 | 80429056 |
| Owner | 2 | 0.708 | 846.347 | 886.184 | 74137600 |
| Owner | 3 | 2.094 | 757.233 | 811.815 | 74780672 |

Each produced 2,952,450 UTF-8 bytes and SHA-256
`dda60c611426db35a65fb54e8f7921fcb843e5516130a6060c2c96465fba2e74`.
The owner includes sanitation and application budget checks that the synthetic
control does not. Runs share one process, allocator and host load. The source
and compiled fixture rejected over-limit captures, generated ranges and long
expression work; the compiled fixture also observed zero trap requests for
file/URL partial lookup attempts. These are representative rejection cases,
not proof of the entire safety matrix.

## Common compiled HTTP workload

`TMPDIR` pointed at the ext4 cache directory for both executables. In three
serial repetitions each, after five warmup cycles, 20 measured cycles performed
health and settings reads, settings write, list create/read/update, individual
subscriber create/read/update/delete, and list delete. The separate restart
preserved the final setting. The candidate-only template cycle creates a tx
record, fetches its preview, updates and deletes it; the M2.2 control has no
such route. It has five warmups and 20 samples per repetition.

| Control | 3 | 22.808 | 370.365 | — |
| Candidate | 1 | 21.017 | 348.114 | 7.122 |
| Candidate | 2 | 22.035 | 347.651 | 9.844 |
| Candidate | 3 | 20.244 | 372.281 | 9.328 |

The protected SMTP diagnostic sent one message from each compiled executable
to invocation-owned Mailpit. The captured recipients and subjects matched,
the two message IDs were distinct, and the HTML bodies matched after whitespace
normalization. This checks the synthetic empty-logo case only, not general
SMTP compatibility or delivery.

The M1.8 acceptance remains incomplete: bounded Markdown rendering, the full
source/compiled adversarial and authorization matrix, and final broader gates
still need work. The measurements above do not change that status.
