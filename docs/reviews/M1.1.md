The [lead decision](https://github.com/iglo-tech/iglo.email/pull/9#issuecomment-6464858024) is recorded as D25: non-reused automatic identities, no explicit old/lower IDs, nullable bounce linkage for external transactional recipients, and documented retention-safe references.
