## Behavior to demonstrate

Drive everything through the public HTTP API (events, sequences, enrollments, subscribers, templates, unsubscribe) against `dist/iglo.email`, with a real or sink SMTP server. Create the tx templates and all nine emails of the worked-example table as sequences. Replace each wait with seconds-scale durations so a full run takes a few minutes at most. Check each email by its received message (recipient, subject, a body marker), not by internal tables.

1. **Prepaid flow.** `order_paid` (prepaid) sends "Credits ready" right away. With no `download_finished`, "Need a hand?" arrives after the wait. With `download_finished`, it doesn't, and the enrollment exits. A second `order_paid` with `order_number: 2` sends "Second pack" once; a third doesn't (`reentry: never`).
2. **Subscription flow.** "Welcome", then "Did the setup work?" only without `connected`. "Your month" sends only when `download_finished` happened within the last 30 days.
3. **Payment failed.** `subscription_past_due` sends step 1. `payment_succeeded` before step 2 exits the enrollment and step 2 never arrives.
4. **Cancel and win-back.** "One question" right away. The win-back step is skipped when `feedback_given` with `reason: "price"` exists, and `subscription_created` exits the sequence.
5. **Marketing consent.** Marketing steps need list permission and respect the frequency cap; set `marketing.min_interval` short enough for the test, and show one cap `defer`. Unsubscribing through the received marketing email's link, and separately through one-click `List-Unsubscribe`, stops later marketing steps and exits the right enrollments, while transactional steps still go out.
6. **Restart.** Stop the process while enrollments are waiting and while a step is due, then restart. Every expected email arrives exactly once.
7. **Edit.** Changing a step's template and wait mid-flight affects only subscribers who haven't reached that step, as 05 says, and deleting a step skips it without breaking anyone.

The design's `subscriber.unsubscribed` webhook is M2.6; leave it out and note it.
