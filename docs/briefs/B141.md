## Goal

One instance service in `src/messaging/` owns the emailer, its SMTP pools and the rate limiter, and decides whose message starts in which slot: classes `transactional` > `marketing` > `campaign`, round-robin across projects within a class, marketing and campaign reserving slots within a 2-second horizon before they claim. Per-campaign pacing keys are removed.

## Behaviour

Section 7 "The dispatcher" exactly: per-push context as push arguments (Message-ID domain, copy-allowed check, hold check of the project or the instance); classes and their members as the table lists; start slots at `1000 / rate` ms; transactional claims first then takes the next free slot; marketing and campaign request up to N slots, get only slots within 2 s, claim at most what was granted with no transaction open while waiting, unused slots returned at once; the campaign cursor advances only over granted recipients; the outbox claim split by kind; synchronous marketing `POST /api/tx` waits at most horizon + 5 s, else stays `queued` with the async answer; rate `0` unpaced but class-ordered and round-robin with a fixed in-flight bound of 200; one process-wide in-flight bound `2 × rate`; wake on enqueue/start, no timer when idle; grants in memory; the shutdown order. The campaign's fixed internal maximum claim starts at 100 and is chosen by measurement (D155). `messaging.max_send_rate` is the effective rate until B145 adds SES's `MaxSendRate`. Projects never build their own emailer. Never add a marketing retry.
