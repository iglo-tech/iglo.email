## Lead decision (record as D96 in `docs/design/09-decisions.md`, lead, 2026-11-12)

The three open points in plan section 5 are lead decisions, not owner items:
- **No retries.** `playwright.config.js` keeps `retries: 0`; a flaky test is fixed, never retried. Reason: a retry hides at-most-once and timing bugs, the exact failures this suite exists to catch.
- **A tag gate, not a merge gate.** The e2e job runs in `release.yml` (tag pushes and manual dry runs) and must pass before `publish`. CI stays manual-only and is still not a merge gate for PRs (AGENTS.md, D90). Reason: releases are rare and must be proven; merges are checked locally by worker and reviewer.
- **The restore-hold instance runs every time** (`operations.e2e.js`, B66). Reason: D67 is release-critical, and the extra instance costs seconds.
