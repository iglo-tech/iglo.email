## Goal

An in-house SigV4 signer on Web Crypto calls SES v2 `GetAccount` with the instance's read-only IAM credentials and keeps an in-memory snapshot on the injected clock. A forecast answers whether the busiest 24 hours around a candidate campaign fit the quota, served by `GET /api/sending/capacity` on every project host, exposed in MCP and OpenAPI, and shown on the campaign page.
